FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from accountants and implementation consultants.

Q
What happens when a customer pays an advance before the invoice is issued? Can Odoo track this without the module?
A
In standard Odoo (without this module), you would need to manually create a journal entry to debit the bank account and credit a liability account of your choice - there is no dedicated advance payment document or workflow. This means no status tracking, no linkage to future invoices, and no automated settlement. The COA Customer Advance Payments module introduces a dedicated Advance Payment model with its own form, list view, status machine (Draft → Confirmed → Settled), and automatic journal entry generation - eliminating all manual steps and ensuring the advance is properly tracked as a liability until the delivery obligation is fulfilled.
Q
What are the exact accounting entries generated by the module, and which accounts are used?
A
The module generates two journal entries during the lifecycle of an advance:

1. On Advance Confirmation:
Dr. Bank / Cash Account            [Advance Amount]
Cr. Customer Advance Liability  [Advance Amount]

2. On Settlement (via Wizard):
Dr. Customer Advance Liability  [Settlement Amount]
Cr. Accounts Receivable         [Settlement Amount]

The Customer Advance Liability account and Bank/Cash account are configurable in Accounting → Settings. The Accounts Receivable account is taken from the customer's account payable/receivable property. If multi-currency is used, an additional FX gain or loss line may be generated automatically.
Q
Can one advance payment be settled against multiple invoices, or can one invoice receive multiple advance payments?
A
Yes - the module fully supports both scenarios. Partial settlement allows a single advance to be applied across multiple invoices over time: each settlement run reduces the advance remaining balance, and the advance status changes from Confirmed to Partially Settled until the balance reaches zero, at which point it becomes Fully Settled. Similarly, a single invoice can have multiple advances applied to it - for example, a 30% advance and a 20% advance from the same customer - and the settlement wizard will show all outstanding advances available for that partner. The remaining invoice balance is updated after each settlement, and the audit trail records every settlement event with date, amount, and the target invoice reference.
