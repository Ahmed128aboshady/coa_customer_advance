Stop Mismanaging Customer Advance Deposits

Standard Odoo books customer down-payments directly against Accounts Receivable, distorting aging reports and requiring messy manual journal entries upon delivery.

Without COA Customer Advance Payments

  • Customer advances credited directly to AR, masking actual customer creditworthiness.
  • Accountants must manually create debit/credit memos upon invoice issuance.
  • Forgotten customer deposits lead to double-billing and embarrassing customer disputes.
  • No segregated balance sheet liability account for customer unearned revenue.

With COA Customer Advance Payments

  • Automated Dr. Bank / Cr. Customer Advance Liability entries upon payment receipt.
  • 1-click settlement wizard reconciles deposit directly against open invoice balance.
  • Real-time outstanding advance notifications displayed prominently on customer invoices.
  • IFRS 15 & GAAP compliant recognition of customer advance obligations.

Core Customer Advance Management Capabilities

Built for finance teams needing strict accounting compliance for pre-orders and down-payments.

Automated Liability Posting

Customer advances post directly to designated Current Liability accounts instead of Accounts Receivable, keeping financial statements compliant.

1-Click Invoice Settlement

When final invoices are posted, open the settlement wizard to apply available advance funds against the invoice balance in seconds.

Sale Order Integration

Register advance payments directly from Quotations or Sale Orders. The advance amount carries through the order lifecycle automatically.

Multi-Currency & FX Engine

Accept deposits in foreign currencies with automated exchange rate conversion and realized FX gain/loss tracking upon settlement.

Partial Settlement Support

Flexibly apply advance deposits across multiple phased deliveries and milestone invoices until the deposit is fully exhausted.

End-to-End Audit Trail

Complete audit trail linking the initial payment receipt, deposit liability journal entry, and final invoice reconciliation.

Accounting Flow

The Customer Advance Accounting Lifecycle

Compliant double-entry bookkeeping from initial deposit to final invoice clearing.

Step 01
Register Advance Receipt

Customer pays down-payment. Auto entry: Dr. Bank / Cr. Customer Advance Liability.

Posting: Liability Recorded
Step 02
Fulfill & Post Invoice

Deliver products and post the customer invoice: Dr. Accounts Receivable / Cr. Revenue.

Status: Invoice Open & Pending
Step 03
Launch Settlement Wizard

Click 'Settle Advance' on invoice. The wizard detects available advance balance.

Action: 1-Click Settlement
Step 04
Auto Clearing & Recon

System posts Dr. Advance Liability / Cr. AR and automatically reconciles invoice balance.

Result: Paid & Fully Reconciled

Frequently Asked Questions

What accounting entries are generated when receiving an advance?

The module records: Debit Bank/Cash and Credit Customer Advance Liability. This ensures the advance is correctly shown as a current liability rather than a negative receivable.

Can one advance payment be settled against multiple invoices?

Yes. The settlement wizard supports partial deductions, allowing you to settle an advance across multiple milestone deliveries or recurring invoices.

Can sales representatives register advances without full accounting rights?

Yes. Sales users can register customer advance payments directly from confirmed Sale Orders within pre-configured permissions.
